## Outcome

The domain and loop groundwork landed on `feat/m2-run-aggregate` (draft PR #41): the `Run` aggregate with a read-only `taints` view and `addTaint`, the Hive journal and its registration hook, the `application/agent/` seam types, the section 9 `runAgent` loop with per-invocation child tokens and non-throwing truncation, fail-closed defaults and their DI bindings.

Step 7 then landed on top of it: `chat` mode end-to-end with model-call journaling. `JournalingLlm` writes a `modelStarted` record and flushes it before every provider call ([ADR-0012](../adr/0012-model-call-journaling.md)); `AgentDepsFactory.forRun` hands out the decorated dependencies. Covered by `runtime/core/test/application/chat_end_to_end_test.dart`, `runtime/core/test/application/journaling_llm_test.dart`, and the extended `runtime/core/test/infrastructure/hive_journal_test.dart` and `runtime/core/test/di/injection_test.dart`.

Postponed within M2: the application service that binds the loop to `RunBloc` and `StatePublisher` (including transcript persistence), section 14 configuration and the interactive approval channel, and section 13 untrusted prompt and tool-output wrapping. The section 2.8 metrics are postponed to M4 because there is no daemon to measure yet.

Decisions the specification does not settle are recorded as Q7–Q14 in [open-questions.md](../spec/open-questions.md); `docs/spec/domain.md` gained pointers to Q7, Q8, and Q14, and ADR-0012 implementation notes in sections 8.5 and 9. Q12 records the observed gap that an approval refusal is not journalled; Q13 records that the loop does not journal model calls, which section 8.10 rule 2 demands, and is resolved in Step 7 by [ADR-0012](../adr/0012-model-call-journaling.md); Q14 records that a timed-out call is kept as `failed`.
